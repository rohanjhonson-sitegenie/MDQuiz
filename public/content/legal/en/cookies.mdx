# Cookie Policy

**Effective Date:** January 18, 2025

## What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website. They help us provide a better user experience and understand how you use our service.

## Types of Cookies We Use

### Essential Cookies
Required for basic website functionality:
- Authentication and security
- Shopping cart and checkout
- Load balancing and performance
- User preferences

### Analytics Cookies
Help us understand website usage:
- Page views and user behavior
- Popular content and features
- Performance metrics
- Error tracking

### Functional Cookies
Enhance your experience:
- Language preferences
- Theme settings
- Recently viewed items
- Saved preferences

### Marketing Cookies
Used for advertising and personalization:
- Targeted advertisements
- Social media integration
- Campaign effectiveness
- Personalized content

## Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party services that set their own cookies:
- Google Analytics
- Social media platforms
- Payment processors
- Content delivery networks

## Managing Cookies

### Browser Settings
You can control cookies through your browser settings:
- **Chrome:** Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies
- **Firefox:** Options > Privacy & Security > Cookies
- **Safari:** Preferences > Privacy > Cookies
- **Edge:** Settings > Privacy and services > Cookies

### Cookie Preferences
You can manage your cookie preferences through our cookie consent banner or by contacting us.

## Impact of Disabling Cookies

Disabling cookies may affect:
- Website functionality
- User experience
- Personalized features
- Analytics and improvements

## Cookie Retention

Different cookies have different retention periods:
- **Session cookies:** Deleted when you close your browser
- **Persistent cookies:** Stored for a specified period
- **Third-party cookies:** Controlled by respective third parties
