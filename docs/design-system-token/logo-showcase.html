Logo Anatomy

The CTRC logo consists of three key elements that work together to create a cohesive brand identity.

1. Icon Mark

The hexagonal brackets with forward slashes represent code, precision, and the technical nature of robotics.

2. Typography

Bold, industrial letterforms convey strength and authority in STEM education.

3. Caution Stripes

The signature yellow diagonal stripes reinforce the "Caution Tape" brand identity with a gradient from #F9D900 to #F7EF72.
